Cancellation & Refunds

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · tuvy inc

This page explains when you can cancel a Tuvy visit or plan for free, when a fee applies, and how refunds work. It applies to the Tuvy app and should be read with our Terms of Service. Before you confirm any cancellation, the app shows whether a fee applies and how much it is. If the app could not show you a fee, you are not charged one.

1. Cancelling a visit

A caregiver who accepts your visit plans their day around it and may already be travelling to the home, so a fee can apply once that journey is real. Cancelling is always free when:

  • no caregiver has accepted the visit yet;
  • the caregiver has not yet set off and the visit is more than 2 hours away;
  • the caregiver is more than 30 minutes past the arrival time we promised you, or their trip has visibly stalled;
  • the caregiver or Tuvy cancels the visit; or
  • you close your Tuvy account (upcoming visits are cancelled without a fee).

A fee may apply when:

  • A visit you asked for now (as soon as possible, or today): cancelling is free for 2 minutes after a caregiver accepts and until they set off. Once they are on the way, the fee is 40% of the visit price.
  • A service booked for a set time: if the caregiver is on the way, or the visit starts within 2 hours, the fee is 50% of the visit price.
  • A video consultation: free before a clinician accepts and for 2 minutes after; after that, the fee is 50% of the price.
  • Nobody at the home: if the caregiver arrives and cannot reach the care recipient, the visit may be charged in full.

Most of each cancellation fee goes to the caregiver for the time and travel they gave up. All prices and fees include GST.

2. Plans

  • Free trial. Monthly plans start with a 30-day free trial. Nothing is charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel at any time during it at no cost.
  • Cancelling a plan. You can cancel at any time, with no penalty. No further payments are taken after you cancel. The period you have already paid for is not refunded, except as described in section 3.
  • Changing a plan. If you move to a different plan, your current plan runs until the end of the period you have paid for and the new plan starts after it.
  • After a plan ends you can still request visits and pay for each one.

3. Refunds

We refund what you paid for a visit or a period of service when:

  • you were charged for a visit that did not take place and was not cancelled by you inside a fee window above;
  • you were charged twice, or charged a wrong amount; or
  • we are unable to provide the service you paid for.

How to ask. Email admin@tuvy.health within 30 days of the charge, with the phone number on your account, the date of the visit or charge, and what went wrong. Your payment receipt is in the app, on the visit report.

How it is paid. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method: card, UPI, net banking or wallet. We start the refund within 2 working days of approving it; your bank usually takes 5–7 working days after that to show it. A visit paid in cash is refunded by bank transfer or UPI to an account you give us.

4. Paying in cash

Where online payment is not yet available, visits are paid in cash to the caregiver when they arrive, and the same cancellation and refund rules apply. Any cancellation fee for a cash visit is collected by the caregiver.
