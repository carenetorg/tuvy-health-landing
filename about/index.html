About Tuvy

Millions of us live a long way from the parents who raised us. We can send money and we can call, but we can’t see how the afternoon actually went. Did someone come? Was she eating? Did anyone notice he seemed tired?

Tuvy exists so you don’t have to wonder. Not with more phone calls, but with a trusted person at your parent’s door, and a true record of every visit on your phone.

What we do

A care professional we have vetted visits your parent at home. We confirm who they are at the door, record what happened during the visit, and send you a sealed account of it: the readings taken, the time on site, and a record that can’t be changed afterwards.

The same caregiver stays with your family for 90 days at a time, because a familiar face is most of what makes care feel like care.

Two apps

  • Tuvy — for you, your family and your parent. Book visits, follow one as it happens, and read what was recorded.
  • Tuvy Pro — for care professionals. Onboarding, credential checks, visits and earnings.

Where we are

Care happens at your parent’s home in India. Families are wherever life has taken them. Our services run on Google Cloud in the United States, which we state plainly in the Privacy Policy, because you deserve to know where your parent’s health data lives.

Honestly, early

Tuvy is a young company, and this page will grow as we do. We would rather earn your trust than ask for it, so we tell you how things actually work and what we don’t do yet. The Data & Security page lists both, including the parts most companies leave out.

If you are worried about a parent and not sure where to start, write to us. We will help you work out what they need.
