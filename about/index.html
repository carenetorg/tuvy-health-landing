About Tuvy

Millions of adult children live a long way from ageing parents. They can send money and they can call, but they cannot see how the afternoon actually went. Tuvy exists to close that gap — not with more phone calls, but with a verified record of real visits.

What we do

A care professional visits the parent at home. Tuvy verifies who that person is, records what happened during the visit, and gives the family a tamper-evident account of it — vitals taken, time on site, and a SHA-256 certificate the record cannot be altered behind.

The same caregiver stays with a family for three to six months, because continuity is most of what makes care feel like care.

Two apps

  • Tuvy — for families and the person being cared for. Schedule visits, follow one live, read what was recorded.
  • Tuvy Provider — for care professionals. Onboarding, credential checks, visits and payouts.

Where we are

Care happens in India. Families are wherever they are. Our services run on Google Cloud in the United States, which we state plainly in the Privacy Policy because it is a real thing to know about where health data lives.
