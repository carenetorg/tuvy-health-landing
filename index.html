FAQ

Common questions

How are Tuvy caregivers verified?

Every caregiver passes a layered Trust Stack: government-ID document verification, INC professional registry checks, mandatory police clearance, an enrolled face photo, and a final manual audit by our Operations team. A caregiver needs a Credibility Score of 80 or higher to enter the matching pool.

How do I know a visit actually happened?

Each visit requires a GPS geofence check-in, a patient-issued OTP, and a dual face scan matched against enrolled biometric templates. Vitals and photo proof stream to your family dashboard in real time, and a SHA-256 tamper-proof certificate is issued the moment the visit is checked out.

Will the same caregiver visit every time?

Yes. Our route-first matching engine locks in one caregiver per family for 90–180 days, with rescheduling only by mutual consent, so your parent builds a relationship with a familiar face rather than a rotating pool of strangers.

Can more than one family member stay involved?

Yes. Tuvy supports a Family Unit structure with role-based access, so multiple family members can be securely invited to view care updates, receive audit certificates, and stay on the same page.

What happens if something urgent comes up during a visit?

Plus and Elite plans include a defined GP escalation path, and every family has visibility into an Operations-backed escalation chain so a concern raised by a caregiver reaches the right person quickly.
