FAQ

Common questions

I’m not sure what kind of care they need. Where do I start?

Register for free, then start with a single home visit. A caregiver checks in on them, takes their vitals, and you get a report in the app the same day. From there you can add regular visits, longer attendant shifts or a video consultation as you learn what helps. If you would rather talk it through first, write to us at admin@tuvy.health and tell us a little about them.

How are Tuvy caregivers verified?

Every caregiver goes through five checks: a government ID, their qualification documents, a nursing or medical council registration where their role requires one, an enrolled face photo, and an in-person ID check. We also ask for a police clearance certificate, which our care team reviews. A caregiver is matched with a family only after our team has verified and approved them.

How do I know a visit actually happened?

Every visit is confirmed three ways at the door, and you get a sealed record when it ends. Check-in is refused if the caregiver’s phone is clearly away from the home; the visit needs a 4-digit code that your loved one and your family both receive in the app, which they read out or you share with the caregiver; and both people at the door take a face scan. Each scan is compared, on our own servers, with the face photo that person enrolled; a mismatch stops the visit. The scans taken at the door are checked and not kept — only the result is recorded. The enrolled photo is kept until consent is withdrawn or the account is deleted. The check cannot yet tell a live face from a printed photograph. Vitals and photos reach your family in the app as they are recorded, and a sealed record that can’t be altered is issued when the visit ends.

Will the same caregiver visit every time?

Yes. One caregiver stays with your loved one for 90 days, changed only when both sides agree, so they get to know a familiar face instead of a rotating pool of strangers.

Can more than one family member stay involved?

Yes. Invite siblings or relatives to see care updates and visit records. Your loved one decides what each person can see.

What happens if something urgent comes up during a visit?

Your loved one or your family can ask for help from inside the app; the request reaches the caregiver and our care team, who follow it up. Tuvy is not an emergency service — in an emergency, call 108 or 112 first.
