Service Delivery

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · tuvy inc

Tuvy sells in-home care visits, not physical goods: nothing is shipped. Each service is delivered in person, at the care recipient's home in India, by a care professional approved by Tuvy. Reports and receipts are delivered in the Tuvy app.

1. Booking a visit

  • As soon as possible, today or tomorrow. Ask for a visit from the app and we offer it to approved caregivers near the home straight away.
  • At a set time. Book a service at least 2 hours ahead and up to 30 days ahead. Times are in India Standard Time, the care recipient's clock. Some services can only be booked during set hours, which the app shows.
  • The price is shown, including GST, before you confirm.

2. Matching a caregiver

  • Your visit is offered first to your family's regular caregiver, if you have one, and then to other approved caregivers nearby.
  • When a caregiver accepts, the app shows who is coming. For regular care, the same caregiver is matched to your family for an ongoing period.
  • If nobody nearby is free straight away, our care team places the visit by hand and tells you in the app. We do not cancel a visit because no caregiver has accepted it yet.

3. The visit

  • When the caregiver sets off, you can follow them on a live map with an arrival estimate.
  • At the door the caregiver checks in at the home, with a door code from the care recipient and a face check, so you know the right person arrived.
  • Most visits last 30 to 60 minutes; some services are longer, and attendant shifts of 8, 12 or 24 hours are available. The length is shown when you book.
  • During the visit, readings and notes appear in the app as the caregiver records them.

4. After the visit

  • A visit report: what was done, the readings taken, and the check-in proof.
  • A payment receipt (PDF), usually within a minute of the visit closing.
  • For a plan, your plan details and payments are under Plan & billing in the app.

5. If a visit cannot go ahead

If a caregiver cancels, or we cannot provide a visit you paid for, you are not charged for it and anything already paid is refunded. See Cancellation & Refunds.
