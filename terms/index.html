1. What Tuvy is

Tuvy arranges in-home care visits for a person who needs care (the "care recipient"), usually on behalf of family members who live elsewhere. Care professionals visit the care recipient at home, record what happened, and the family can follow the visit in the app.

Not a medical device, not medical advice, not an emergency service

Tuvy and Tuvy Pro are not medical devices. They do not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and nothing shown in either app — readings, trends, summaries or reports — is medical advice. Talk to a doctor about any health decision.

Tuvy is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency services — in India, 108 for an ambulance or 112 — and do not wait for a reply from Tuvy.

5. Visits, payments and cancellations

  • Paying for visits. Online plan sign-up and card payment are not open yet. Until they are, visits are billed one at a time and paid in cash to the caregiver when they arrive. When online plans and payments become available, their price and terms will be shown in the app before you agree to them.
  • Cancelling. Before you confirm a cancellation, the app shows whether a fee applies. Cancelling is free shortly after a caregiver accepts and until the caregiver sets off. Once the caregiver is on the way, a cancellation fee — a share of the visit price — may apply, because their journey is real. There is no fee if the caregiver is well past the promised arrival time or their trip has visibly stalled, or if the caregiver or Tuvy cancels.
  • Caregiver earnings. Care professionals see their earnings for completed visits in Tuvy Pro and add the bank account or UPI ID they want to be paid to.
