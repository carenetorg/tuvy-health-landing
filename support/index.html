Support

We answer every message, usually within one working day (India time). Write to admin@tuvy.health and tell us which app you use and the phone number on your account.

In a medical emergency, do not wait for us. In India call 108 (ambulance) or 112. Elsewhere, call your local emergency number. Tuvy is not an emergency service.

Tuvy — for families

  • Signing in. Enter your mobile number with its country code and type the six-digit code we send you by SMS.
  • Visits. Upcoming visits are on Home and on the Schedule tab. Times are shown in your parent's time zone (India Standard Time).
  • Apple Health. Optional, and read-only. Connect it from the menu under My devices; turn it off any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Tuvy.
  • Plans and payments. Plans pay for in-person care visits and are paid on our payment provider's secure page. For a receipt or a billing question, email us.

Tuvy Pro — for care professionals

  • Joining. Tuvy Pro is for professionals approved by Tuvy. Sign in with your mobile number and complete the application; we review your documents before your first visit.
  • Location. Tuvy Pro shares your location only between Start trip and the end of a visit, so the family can see you arriving. iOS shows the location indicator while it does.
  • Payouts. Earnings for completed visits are paid monthly to the bank account you add in the app.

Your account and data

  • Delete your account in either app: Profile → Delete account. This is permanent.
