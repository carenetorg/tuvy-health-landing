Support

We answer every message, usually within one working day (India time). Write to admin@tuvy.health and tell us which app you use and the phone number on your account.

In a medical emergency, do not wait for us. In India call 108 (ambulance) or 112. Elsewhere, call your local emergency number. Tuvy is not an emergency service, and Tuvy and Tuvy Pro are not medical devices: nothing in either app is medical advice.

Tuvy — for families

  • Signing in. Enter your mobile number with its country code and type the six-digit code we send you by SMS.
  • Finding your way. The tabs are Today, Visits, Book, Health and Care team. If you use Tuvy for yourself rather than for a parent, they are Today, My numbers and Help. Your profile, Plan & billing and My profile are behind the round profile button at the top right.
  • Visits. The next visit is on Today, and every booked visit is on the Visits tab. Times are shown in your parent's time zone (India Standard Time).
  • Apple Health. Optional, and read-only. Connect it from the Health tab (My numbers, if you use Tuvy for yourself), under Watch & activity; turn it off any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Tuvy.
  • Plans and payments. Plans pay for in-person care visits and are paid on our payment provider's secure page. Manage yours under your profile (top right) → Plan & billing. For a receipt or a billing question, email us.

Tuvy Pro — for care professionals

  • Joining. Tuvy Pro is for professionals approved by Tuvy. Sign in with your mobile number and complete the application; we review your documents before your first visit.
  • Finding your way. The tabs are Today, Schedule, Patients and Earnings. Your profile is the button at the top right of Today.
  • Location. While Tuvy Pro is open on your screen, it uses your location to offer you visits nearby and to give families an arrival estimate. In the background it uses your location only during a visit trip — from I'm on my way until you tap You've arrived — check in at the door — so the family can see you arriving. iOS shows the location indicator, and Android a "Tuvy trip" notification, while it does. You can turn location off in your phone's settings.
  • Payouts. Earnings for completed visits are paid monthly to the bank account or UPI ID you add in the app.

Your account and data

  • Delete your account from inside the app. This is permanent.
    • Tuvy: your profile (top right) → My profile → scroll to the bottom → Delete account.
    • Tuvy Pro, approved: your profile (top right of Today) → scroll to the bottom → Delete account.
    • Tuvy Pro, application still under review: on the application status screen, scroll to the bottom → Delete account.
  • Report a problem or a person. Report anything inappropriate or unsafe — a caregiver, a family member, a note or a photo — from inside the app, or email admin@tuvy.health. We review every report, and can block the person from being matched with you again.
